Bot IPL Rosewood

Bot IPL Rosewood is a private WhatsApp automation used internally by the Rosewood neighborhood committee to collect residents' IPL (Iuran Pemeliharaan Lingkungan / neighborhood dues) payment proof photos and committee expense records, and log them into a private Google Sheet for bookkeeping.

It is not a public product or service — it serves a single residential community and is operated by that community's own committee.

See our Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.
