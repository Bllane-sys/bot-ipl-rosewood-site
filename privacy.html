Privacy Policy — Bot IPL Rosewood

Last updated: September 2026

Bot IPL Rosewood ("the App") is a private WhatsApp automation operated for a single residential community (the "Rosewood" neighborhood) to collect resident dues payment proofs and committee expense records. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

What data we collect

When a resident or committee member messages the App's WhatsApp number, we collect: the sender's WhatsApp number, the name and house block/number they provide during self-registration, any photo they send as payment or expense proof, and any accompanying text description.

How we use it

Photos are uploaded to a private Google Drive folder, and the associated details (name, house number, amount, description, and a link to the uploaded photo) are appended as a row to a private Google Sheet. Both are accessible only to the neighborhood committee, for the sole purpose of tracking who has paid dues or submitted an expense.

Google API scope used

The App uses the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This scope only grants the App access to files and folders that the App itself creates — it cannot see, read, or modify any other files in the connected Google account's Drive. The App also uses the Google Sheets API to append rows to one specific, pre-existing spreadsheet that the community's committee owns and controls.

Data retention and sharing

Data is retained in the Drive folder and Sheet indefinitely, as a financial record for the committee. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any third party. Data is not used for advertising.

Data deletion

A resident may request removal of their submitted data at any time by contacting the committee at the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ciresrosewood@gmail.com.
